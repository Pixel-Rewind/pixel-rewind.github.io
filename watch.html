Good to know

What can I watch?

Online matches recorded since the server started keeping match recordings. Matches played with computer players, and Princess Rush matches, may not replay exactly; the list still shows them.

Skip idle time, the announcer, 2×

Skip idle time (on) jumps over the waits between moves while the board is still. Wait for the announcer (off) makes each phase wait until the announcer has finished speaking, as the original did. 2× plays at double speed. You can drag the time bar to any point of the match.

Save as video

The page can render the match to an MP4 file, in your browser, as fast as your device manages (a few times faster than the match itself in Chrome). Chrome and Edge are quickest; Firefox makes VP9 video, which takes several times the match’s length. Nothing is uploaded.

It does not work in my browser

Use a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer or a recent phone. The page needs WebAssembly and, for sound, AudioWorklet. Importing the PC version’s folder needs a browser that can pick a folder (everything but some phone browsers; on those, import a .n-gage file instead). Private windows may refuse to keep the game in storage: you would import it again every visit.
