Accounts

Not open yet

There is nothing to sign up for at the moment. If you came here from Create an account in the app, this is the right place — it just isn't ready.

You do not need an account to play. Importing a game and playing it on your own works entirely offline, and always will. An account is only for the online features that some games have.

What an account will be for

  • A player name of your own.
  • Matchmaking, and matches against other people.
  • One account across every game the app plays.

It will cost nothing.

When

No date. Online play itself works, but sign-up is not something worth opening before it can be supported properly. When it opens it will be on this page, and the app will not need an update for that to happen — the button you pressed already points here.

If you already have a log-in

Some games connect to community-run servers that existed before this app did. If you already have credentials for one, the app's I already have an account option on the same screen takes them.
