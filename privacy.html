Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Pixel Rewind Player does not collect, profile or sell anything about you. This page sets out exactly what the app stores, what leaves your device, and what it never touches.

The short version. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It requests one Android permission, INTERNET, and uses it only if you choose to play online. Games you import and settings you change stay on your device.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the Pixel Rewind Player application for Android and Linux (“the app”) and this website. In this policy, “we” means the developer of Pixel Rewind Player.

What the app stores on your device

All of the following is held in the app's own private storage on your device. None of it is transmitted to us, and we have no ability to read it.

  • Game files you import. When you point the app at a game, it copies the files it needs into its own storage so it can run them. These files are never uploaded.
  • Your settings. Screen orientation, layout size, control choices, language, and the other options on the settings screen.
  • Game saves and progress. Whatever the game itself writes, kept where that game expects it.
  • Online sign-in details, if you use online play. A user name and password for the game server, stored on the device so you do not have to type them each time.

What leaves your device

If you only play on your own, nothing does. The app makes no network connections at all unless you use an online feature of a game.

If you do play online, the app connects directly to a game server — for games whose original servers no longer exist, that is a community-run replacement. In the course of that connection:

  • The user name you chose is sent to the server, so other players can see who they are playing against.
  • Your password is sent to the server to sign you in.
  • The server necessarily sees your device's IP address, as any internet server does.
  • Game traffic for the match itself — moves, results and similar — passes between you and the server.

That server is operated separately from the app, and its operator decides what it records. If you use a server you were given by someone else, its handling of your data is between you and whoever runs it.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No crash or diagnostic reporting.
  • No third-party libraries, software development kits or services embedded in the app.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, call logs or messages. The app does not request those permissions and cannot use them.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.

Android permissions

The app declares exactly one permission:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — used solely to reach a game server when you play online.

Choosing a game to import uses the Android system file picker, which grants the app access only to the file or folder you pick, at the moment you pick it.

Accounts

Accounts on this website are not open yet. If they are introduced later, this policy will be updated before any sign-up is possible, and will say what is collected and why.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because it collects nothing, there is nothing about a child for us to hold or delete.

Keeping your information

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain, secure or destroy. The data described above lives on your own device, protected by your device's own security and by Android's separation between apps.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored, including imported games, settings and saves. On Android you can also clear the app's data from Settings without uninstalling. On Linux, deleting the app's data directory has the same effect.

If you have used an online server, ask that server's operator about anything it holds; we cannot act on their behalf.

This website

This site is a set of static pages with no accounts, no comments and no forms. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It loads a web font from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests that font file from Google's servers and Google will see the IP address making the request; nothing else is sent. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep server logs of requests as part of operating the service.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes affecting what the app collects will be described here rather than made quietly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@pixelrewind.app.
