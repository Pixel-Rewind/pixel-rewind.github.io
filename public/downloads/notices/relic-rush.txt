ARTWORK CREDITS - RELIC RUSH

Every sprite in this game is from the Super Retro World collection, used under its licence,
which permits commercial use and asks for credit. The authors are:

    Gif        (@gif_not_jif)
    Noiracide  (@Noiracide)
    Romi       (@DessRomaric)

Packs used:

    Super Retro World - Character Pack   the two heroes and the relic chests
    Super Retro World - Interior Pack    the floor, the home rugs, the wall and the braziers

What was done to them: sprites were cut out of the pack sheets; one stone tile was recoloured
into the three board states, each claimed one given a gold rim; and a brazier was composed
from the pack's stone bowl and three frames of its fire animation. assets/manifest.json records
the source file and the exact crop behind every sprite. The originals are not redistributed
here, only this game's own cut-down artwork, baked into relicrush.pak.

The pack licence also asks that assets not be distributed directly, even modified. Nothing in
this folder is offered as an asset pack or as a substitute for buying one; please support the
authors by buying the packs if you want to use this artwork in your own project.

the artwork, which stays under the Super Retro World licence above.
